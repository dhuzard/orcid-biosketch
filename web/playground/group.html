    ENCRYPTED TEAM PACKAGE Hand a group from your research network to a teammate
    What a team package contains

    One group from the research network stored in this browser: its name and description, each member’s public ORCID record as last read into your network, and your local roles, expertise, contributions, selected project outputs and requests. It is encrypted in this browser with a password you choose (PBKDF2-SHA256, AES-GCM-256); nothing is uploaded. Anyone who has both the file and the password can read all of it, so send the password by a different channel. The people listed have not confirmed their membership, roles or contributions.

    Open a team package

    Uses the public ORCID API. A link between two members means only that both public records list the same DOI; it does not prove collaboration, authorship roles or consent to this group, and records may be incomplete.
