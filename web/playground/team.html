IN DEVELOPMENT · NOT YET ENABLED ON THE PUBLIC SITE

Team workspaces

Planned: a private page for each team, which only people you invite, who sign in with their own ORCID iD and accept, can open. Cloud Teams are not confirmed active on this site; the browser-local research network and encrypted team packages work without them.

When Teams are activated, team data (roles, contributions, notes, requests, selected outputs) is stored in Google Cloud Firestore in the EU and the access rules allow only the team's active members to read it. Signing in shows that you control an ORCID account; it does not verify your affiliations or credentials, and membership is what the team records, not an ORCID assertion. Public ORCID records are read live and are not copied. Leaving a team removes your access; you can delete anything recorded about you.
