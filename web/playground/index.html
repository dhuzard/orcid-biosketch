A public ORCID record is a set of assertions made by the researcher and their sources. Every output re-expresses those assertions; none of them verifies identity, authorship or affiliations.

MULTI-ORCID

Bring your working group together.

Follow researchers and see their new outputs in one feed. Build a working group from a pasted list of ORCID iDs, pick the project's outputs, track requests, and download a team pack with the roster, a deduplicated bibliography and every member's biosketch.

Your network is stored only in this browser. Group membership and roles are curated by you and are not confirmed by the people listed.

Open the research network → See it with sample data Make a shareable group link
  1. Paste ORCID iDsUp to 50 at a time, checked before import.
  2. Curate the groupRoles, expertise and contributions, recorded locally.
  3. Select project outputsDeduplicated across members by DOI.
  4. Download a team packRoster, bibliography and biosketches, filtered by member and date.

Uses the public ORCID API. Outputs only re-express what a record asserts; a valid ORCID iD is not proof of identity, and records may be incomplete or out of date.

Everything runs in your browser. The default research network is browser-local; optional cloud Teams and account sync are not yet enabled on the public site and, if activated later, would store team data in a separate EU cloud database.
